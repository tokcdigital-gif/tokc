FAQ

Questions founders ask us.

Are the documents valid under Nigerian law?

Yes. They’re written for Nigerian businesses, and the legal documents are prepared by our partnering law firm, LawSavvy NG, with Nigerian employment, company and data protection rules in mind.

Can I edit them?

Yes. Every document comes in editable Word, Excel, Google or Canva format, with clear spaces for your details, so you can adapt it to your business.

Do I still need a lawyer?

For everyday use, the templates cover what most small businesses need. For unusual situations, disputes or high-value contracts, you should get advice on your specific case. LawSavvy NG can review or customise any document if you need it.

What’s the difference between the Full Box and Done-for-You?

With the Full Box you get all the templates and fill them in yourself. With Done-for-You, we customise the documents to your business: we fill in your details, align the policies with how you actually work, and set them up ready to use.

Do you train our staff?

Yes. Done-for-You and both structuring packages end with a handover session that walks your team through the new policies, roles and procedures. Further training can be added if you need it.
